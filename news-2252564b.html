New in WoodPrep
CORP26NewOctober 2026
Cabinet Designer — the new cabinet generator
Design a complete carcass from one panel: set the dimensions and the construction, and the parts, hole patterns and part names are generated and checked automatically before anything is drawn. The finished cabinet is placed as a grouped model, ready for the cutting list and CNC output.
ROBBANTNewOctober 2026
Exploded View with Grain Direction and Edge Banding
Creates an exploded copy of the cabinet next to the model, with every part labelled by name, edge banding shown in colour and grain direction marked. The original model is not touched. Ideal for the workshop, for assembly and for checking the parts before production.
Video coming
DXFNewOctober 2026
DXF Export for Special Parts
Exports parts as DXF directly from the 3D model — for curved and shaped parts, or for any machine and service that works from DXF. The files are written into their own folder next to the machine programs.
Video coming
ELZARASNewOctober 2026
Automatic Edge Banding
Assigns edge banding to every part of the cabinet in one step and shows it in colour on the model, so you can check it at a glance. Identical parts are grouped, and the result goes straight into the cutting list.
