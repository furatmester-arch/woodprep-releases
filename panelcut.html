What it does
PANELCUTAdd-on
Cutting maps from the drawing
Reads every part on the selected layer of your DWG and lays them out into optimized cutting maps. No exporting, no retyping — the parts you already drew become the cutting plan.
Add-on
Set up for your own machine
Define the board size you buy, the saw blade thickness (kerf) and the trim allowance on the board edges. The layout is calculated with your real values, so the map matches what the saw will actually cut.
Add-on
Waste and yield figures
Every board is reported with its offcut waste and material utilisation, so you can see before cutting how much of the sheet actually turns into parts — and quote accordingly.
Add-on
Printable summary and maps
Print the summary sheet for the office and the cutting maps for the shop floor. The operator gets a drawing of every board with the parts positioned on it.
How it works

Draw as usual

Keep working the way you do now — the parts on your chosen layer are the input.

Set board, kerf and trim

Enter the sheet size you stock, your saw blade thickness and the edge trim allowance.

Run PANELCUT

The parts are nested onto boards and the cutting maps are generated.

Print and cut

Summary with waste and utilisation figures for the office, cutting maps for the saw.
